# Planning memo: procurement audit

> This pack plans work on **risk indicators to investigate**, drawn from public data. Nothing in it is a finding of wrongdoing by the council, a service, an officer or a supplier.

Built 2026-10-10 18:51 from the risk register **Revision 2** and the auditor's decisions in `auditor-comments.md`.

## Objective

To give assurance on whether the council's purchasing in the period followed its Contract Rules: that the value of a requirement was estimated as a whole [CPR-14, cl. 6.15] and not divided [CPR-15, cl. 6.16], that the competition and notice set for its value band took place [CPR-57, cl. App B row B1] [CPR-58, cl. App B row B1], that exceptions, exclusions, extensions and variations carried the approval the Rules name [CPR-24, cl. 7.3] [CPR-68, cl. App C row D] [CPR-69, cl. App C row E], that awards were approved at the level their value requires [CPR-05, cl. 4.4], and that the records the Rules require are held and published [CPR-08, cl. 5.2.1] [CPR-34, cl. 9.3]. The work is planned for the 10 risks the auditor placed in scope out of 11 in the register.

## Scope

The analysis covers published payments dated 2026-05-01 to 2026-07-31, 3 months: 8,534 payments to 1,083 suppliers, worth £66,778,104.78, out of 10,607 published rows. Three groups of rows were excluded before any test: 2,015 rows whose payee is published as 'Redacted', 12 statutory pension payments and 46 payments to other public bodies. Notices are the 141 Contracts Finder notices for the council. Scope follows the auditor's recorded decisions and nothing else: each risk the auditor approved or overrode is planned at the rating shown in the table below, and a risk the auditor rejected has no control, test or sample. No risk has been added and no rating has been changed in planning.

## Approach

Each flagged item is a reason to look at a record and nothing more. Every data-led test therefore starts by reconciling the sampled suppliers and payments to the council's own records (the contract register, the order and the invoice) and only then moves to document tests against the rule cited in the step. Samples are drawn by the builder from the flagged lists in a fixed order, largest first, so that the draw can be repeated. Where the auditor directed a starting point it is planned as a whole band: the general-procurement windows at the £500,000 band are taken in full, for the split-purchase risk and again for the approval risk the auditor overrode. Signals are kept apart by type of spend: tests on competition and notices are filtered to general procurement; placement-tagged items are planned only under the Appendix C risk, against the rule on individual placements [CPR-71, cl. App C row F]; grant-tagged, premises-tagged and agency-staff-tagged items are in no sample. For agency staff that leaves unexamined one window at the £500,000 band, worth £546,495.30, and one supplier at or above the publication value with no matching notice, paid £1,275,552.15 in the period; the register's risks count general-procurement items only, so whether these are examined is a matter for the auditor. Risks with no data signal are tested by walkthrough and by document tests on a selection the auditor draws in the field from a list the council provides, each named in the requests to the council. The Threshold figures set by the Procurement Legislation are not printed in the Rules, so every control that turns on them says the figure is to be confirmed, and the first step under that risk is to obtain the figures in force for the period. Where the Rules leave a value in no band or state a requirement in two ways, a step establishes which reading the council applies before any record is compared with it.

## Limitations

The published data holds only payments above £500 [CPR-06, cl. 5.1.1], so counts of repeated or related payments are lower bounds. The date is a payment-run timestamp, not an order or invoice date, and the data carries no invoice number, order number or company number. Annualised estimates scale 3 months of spend to a year and may not be typical. The Contracts Finder export holds published notices and is not a contract register: no result has been checked against the council's own register, which was not available to the analysis, and supplier matching is by name only. Every Threshold parameter is null in the rules file [analytics `threshold_source.null_parameters`], so no test was run at those tiers and the plan cannot yet say which contracts sit above them. The committee papers print opinions for earlier audits but no findings, recommendations or agreed actions, so the plan does not rely on what earlier audits found. Samples are targeted at flagged items; a result describes the items examined.
