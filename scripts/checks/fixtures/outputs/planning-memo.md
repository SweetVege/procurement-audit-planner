# Planning memo: procurement audit

> This pack plans work on **risk indicators to investigate**, drawn from public data. Nothing in it is a finding of wrongdoing by the council, a service, an officer or a supplier.

Built 2026-10-10 18:39 from the risk register **Revision 1** and the auditor's decisions in `auditor-comments.md`.

## Objective

To assess whether purchasing followed the value bands in the Rules [CPR-01, cl. 2.1], for the 2 risks the auditor placed in scope.

## Scope

The analysis covered 8,534 payments over 3 months. Split-purchase windows in general procurement total £2,310,961.19.

### Risks in scope (auditor's decisions)

| Risk | Title | Rating | Auditor decision | Tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Purchases may be divided below a value band | Likely (3) × Medium (3) = 9 High | approve | T-01.1 |
| R-02 | Repeated payments of the same amount | Likely (3) × Minor (2) = 6 Moderate | approve | T-02.1 |

### Out of scope

The auditor rejected no risk.

## Approach

Each flagged item is traced to the council's own records before any conclusion is drawn.

### Samples

Samples are targeted at the flagged items, not statistical; results will not be extrapolated. Size follows the risk's score: score 15+ → 25 items; score 8+ → 15 items; score 4+ → 10 items; score 1+ → 5 items; a population at or below that size is taken whole. A census is allowed up to 50 items.
